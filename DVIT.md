# Carry intent across engineering workflows

DVIT provides an intent integrity layer: requirements, rationale, configurations,
decisions and engineering evidence connected with their sources and revisions.
It gives the existing AI a maintained basis for asking what a result means,
which conditions justify it, and what another design change puts back in scope.

OpenTools captures the intent needed for one activity. DVIT extends that context
across dependent reviews: a coverage exclusion can lead back to its configuration
premise; a changed interface can lead to software and checker obligations; a
timing result can be reviewed against the decision and mode it must support.

## Evidence behind the approach

The Computable Design Intent white papers report concrete semiconductor examples:

- Revision-bound source changes became inspectable software, configuration and
  verification review questions.
- Improved graph access recovered an earlier evidence gap and exposed why a
  closure record did not cover the full obligation.
- Combined retrieval and intent context produced an assignable review worksheet.
  Combined and graph-led workflows used 13.5% and 9.0% fewer investigation output
  tokens than matched source retrieval in the second study.

These are demonstrated benefits on a compact semiconductor fixture with simulated
organizational records. The studies distinguish generation demand from total cost;
they do not claim that every connected workflow is cheaper. Their value is a
concrete starting point for evaluating evidence completion, handoff and reuse in
your own engineering environment.

Sources: Akshay Sehgal, *Computable Design Intent: A Versioned Graph for Engineering
Consistency and Evidence Traceability*, v1.3, 24 September 2026; and *When Computable
Design Intent Improves Frontier AI: Evidence Access, Engineering Handoffs, and the
Conditions for Lower Generation Cost*, v1.2, 27 September 2026.

## Connect when available

Use DVIT to retain these relationships as requirements, implementation and
verification evolve. Onboarding can identify the specific context question to
take into that integration. Existing local sources and company systems remain
fully usable.

The DVIT discovery URL will be added when its published destination is available.
Connection uses a separately configured adapter through the
[context interface](CONTEXT_PORT.md); no download or connection is performed by
OpenTools.
