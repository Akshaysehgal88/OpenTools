# Short experiment record

Use this in the existing issue/run directory. Markdown is enough; the optional
JSON helper is useful when exact history/result correspondence is needed.

- **Observed fact and source:** original execution, transaction/window and evidence.
- **Question/decision:** what uncertainty matters and what the answer would change.
- **Plausible explanations:** grounded alternatives, plus acknowledged missing context.
- **One next action:** inspect existing evidence, ask for specific context, propose
  one run, or stop. Explain why it is the cheapest adequate route and what prior
  attempts/artifacts were checked.
- **Controls and intervention:** relevant binaries/configuration, stimulus/seed,
  scheduling/external state and instrumentation; record fixed, intended and
  uncontrolled factors. No universal dimension list is complete for every flow.
- **Required observations:** actual attainable sources, same-transaction identity,
  scope/window and completeness needed to distinguish the explanations.
- **Outcome → next step:** normal alternatives, incomplete evidence, execution
  failure and divergent reproduction. Include what each outcome can and cannot
  establish, and a stopping condition.
- **After the step:** append the unchanged plan reference, actual execution or
  evidence-only route, actual changes, observations, chosen interpretation,
  remaining uncertainty and scoped costs. Preserve failures and unexecuted plans.

Do not rewrite the original hypotheses after seeing the data. A revised experiment
gets a new plan identity and a link to the earlier attempt. An exploratory change
may be useful without reproducing or resolving the original failure.
