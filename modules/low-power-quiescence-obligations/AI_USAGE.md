# AI usage

Load [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) in your current agent. Supply the consumed ownership/
counter sources, shutdown policy and native transition observations.

Example request:

> Review this drain acknowledgement and shutdown. Identify all owners that still
> depend on the domain, preserve count units/bases, and check new admission and
> prerequisites throughout the protected interval. Inspect raw native evidence
> even when the helper agrees; keep missing coverage and physical claims separate.

No AI SDK or DVIT connection is required. The agent may inspect additional native
tools and source relationships beyond the helper. A separately configured context
adapter can add scoped evidence; it does not grant approval or attest to coverage.
