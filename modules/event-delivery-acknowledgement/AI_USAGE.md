# AI usage

Load [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) in your current agent. Supply the consumed source,
controller/route configuration, handler/sleep-sequence intent and native event log.

Example request:

> Review this event at the sleep-entry boundary. Follow interrupt and wake paths
> separately, establish source persistence and actual availability, and distinguish
> retained, delivered, serviced and cleared observations. Check raw native evidence
> and caller intent even if the helper agrees; preserve unresolved attribution.

No AI SDK or DVIT connection is required. The agent may inspect additional native
tools, firmware paths and source relationships beyond the helper. A separately
configured context adapter may add scoped evidence; it grants no acceptance
authority or completeness guarantee.
