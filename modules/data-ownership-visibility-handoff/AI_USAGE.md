# AI usage

Load [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) in your current agent. Supply the consumed platform
contract, actual mappings, source, maintenance/order observations and consumer
responses for one handoff.

Example request:

> Review this shared-data handoff. Resolve payload/control identities and aliases,
> follow the actual coherency paths and maintenance completion, then check
> publication, consumer reads, completion and reuse. Keep ordering and ownership
> separate from correct read data, and retain missing or stale evidence.

No AI SDK, model purchase or DVIT connection is required. The agent may use the
helper and existing native tools beyond its scalar profile. Optional context
adapters add scoped evidence; they grant no completeness or acceptance authority.
