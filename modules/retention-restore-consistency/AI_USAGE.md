# AI usage

Load [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) in your current agent. Supply consumed selection/source
artifacts, the before image, restore history and actual consumer reads.

Example request:

> Review this retained structure as a consumer bundle. Establish payload/metadata
> roles and initialization dependencies from source, then inspect first exposure
> and actual post-wake reads. Distinguish paired retention, safe rebuild and cold
> discard intent. Keep missing coverage and physical qualification separate.

No AI SDK or DVIT connection is required. The agent can use native tools and
source relationships beyond the helper's scalar profile. A separately configured
context adapter may add scoped evidence; it grants no acceptance authority or
completeness guarantee.
