# Observation review handoff

- **Objective:** original obligation, receiving owner, event and observer link.
- **Boundary:** original build/checker, clock/edge, reset, modes and ID/event map.
- **Observation:** offered, accepted, completed and checked identities, enable
  intervals, reset cancellations and window-end pending work; cite source/journal.
- **Native result:** PASS/FAIL and actions, separate from observation validity;
  retain the expected-data model's basis.
- **Conclusion:** known differences, missing evidence and exact supported scope.
- **Action:** retained evidence or proposed correction, owner review and affected
  recheck. No action when evidence answers the question.
- **Limits:** instrumentation/oracle assumptions and adequacy/liveness claims open.

Attach helper inputs and JSON/Markdown if used. Exposure accounting supports the
existing campaign review; it grants no stop or release authority.
